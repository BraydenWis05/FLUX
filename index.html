ORBIT 01 · DEFINITION

UX is more than
designing screens.

UX is about understanding people, and designing better experiences for them. It can be an app, a website, a physical product, a healthcare experience, a car, a service — or something that doesn't exist yet.

It brings together research, technology, design, and problem-solving to figure out what people need, and build things that actually work for them.
