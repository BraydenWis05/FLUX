ORBIT 01

So... what is UX?

UX is about understanding people and designing better experiences for them. It can be an app, a website, a physical product, a healthcare experience, a car, a service — or something that doesn't exist yet.

UX brings together research, technology, design, and problem-solving to figure out what people need, and create experiences that actually work for them.

UX is more than
designing screens.

ORBIT 05 · HOME BASE

Your community for UX.

FLUX is SCAD's UX club and community — a place to learn, connect, collaborate, get feedback, and work with real professionals outside the classroom.

Members have run Figma bootcamps, built a mentorship center, hosted Fluxathon, launched a podcast, organized a product design conference, and taken on real client work through the FLUX design agency.
